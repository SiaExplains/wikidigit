_GoAll.ai's services, prices and claims below come from its own website, read on October 22, 2026. Its client case studies are anonymous, so their numbers can't be checked independently._

GoAll.ai is a small German AI agency that promises early-stage startups "enterprise-level AI without an enterprise budget." It is a two-partner company, legally goall.ai GbR, run by co-CEOs Sheida Mohassesy and Hesam Dehghan. It sells six services, from AI integration to branding. What sets it apart from most agencies is what it publishes: a full price list. That list is not for startups, though. It's for driving schools.

## Six Services, One Pitch: Automate Before You Scale

GoAll.ai's homepage headline, "Vollständig auf KI setzen" ("go all in on AI"), says the company helps early-stage startups integrate AI and automation, "agile, pragmatic and highly scalable." Its [services page](https://goall.ai/de/services) lists six offerings:

- **AI integration and strategy**, from choosing a language model to running it in production
- **Custom automation** of routine work such as lead nurturing, document processing and reporting
- **Web and app development** with Next.js and TypeScript
- **Web design**, **design and brand identity**, and **IT consulting** for founders

None of those six services has a public price or timeline. The way in is a free 30-minute discovery call. That's normal for agency work, where every project is different. It also means a founder can't compare GoAll.ai with a freelancer or a larger shop without talking to them first.

## The Price List Is for Driving Schools

The [pricing page](https://goall.ai/de/pricing) is where GoAll.ai gets specific, and it's a vertical product rather than agency work. The "Fahrschul-Paket" automates a German driving school's back office in seven modules: student sign-up and onboarding, theory classes, lesson booking, progress tracking, invoicing and payments, documents, and instructor and vehicle management. An eighth module, an AI phone receptionist, answers calls.

The full package costs €4,150 to set up and lists at €950 a month. An "early access" discount of 60% for the first ten customers brings that to €380 a month, and GoAll.ai estimates it saves about 62 hours of work a month. Single modules run from €350 to €600 to set up and €36 to €60 a month after the discount. There's no long-term contract. The phone receptionist has no flat fee and is priced by call volume, with a [free ROI calculator](https://goall.ai/de/sprachassistent-rechner) to estimate it.

Two things stand out. Publishing prices at all is unusual for a small AI agency, and it's the most useful page on the site. And the discount is not new: GoAll.ai's own blog announced the 60% offer for the first ten driving schools in June 2025, so "early access" has now run for more than a year.

## Case Studies Without Names

GoAll.ai's [work page](https://goall.ai/de/work) shows two anonymous case studies. In the first, a Series A fintech startup's loan-document review went from four hours to under 35 minutes per application, and its error rate fell from 12% to 0.4%. GoAll.ai built a pipeline on GPT-4o, AWS Lambda and Python, in three weeks plus a two-week parallel run, and humans still make the final lending decision. The page headlines a 70% cut in manual review time, though its own figures, from eight hours a day to under two, work out to about 75%. In the second, a seed-stage SaaS company's AI onboarding assistant cut onboarding support tickets by 45%.

Unnamed clients are common in agency marketing, but they make the numbers impossible to check. The named work is in GoAll.ai's [showcase](https://goall.ai/de/showcase): seven websites and apps, including a photography studio, a Berlin construction firm, the Pomodoro app focus-crew.com, [Emojar](/article/emojar-pivot-264-free-browser-tools) and WikiDigit itself. Several of those belong to people close to GoAll.ai's team, so they show what it can build more than who is paying it.

## Who It's For

If you're a German driving school, GoAll.ai is unusually easy to evaluate: the modules, setup fees and monthly prices are all on one page, and the ROI calculator gives a cost estimate for the phone receptionist in about a minute. If you're a startup, the offer is a small, hands-on team that builds production AI systems and websites. The trade-off is that you'll judge it on a discovery call rather than on public prices or named references.

The bigger bet is the one the whole market is making. As [AI agents start acting on people's behalf](/article/google-io-2026-gemini-agentic-ai), the work moves from demos to plumbing: connecting models to bookings, invoices and phone lines. GoAll.ai is wagering that small businesses will pay someone to do that plumbing for them. The driving-school package is the first place it has put a price on that bet.
