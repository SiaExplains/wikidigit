Anthropic released **Claude Opus 4** and **Claude Sonnet 4** on May 22, 2025, [presenting them](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-4) as models built for coding, advanced reasoning and AI agents that work through long, multi-step tasks.

## What are Claude Opus 4 and Sonnet 4?

They are the fourth generation of Anthropic's Claude models. Opus 4 is the larger, more expensive model meant for the hardest and longest tasks. Sonnet 4, which Anthropic called "a significant upgrade" to Sonnet 3.7, is the faster, cheaper option and, unlike Opus 4, was also available to free Claude users.

## Benchmarks Anthropic published

All figures below are Anthropic's own, from its launch post:

| Benchmark | Opus 4 | Sonnet 4 |
|---|---|---|
| SWE-bench Verified (standard) | 72.5% | 72.7% |
| SWE-bench Verified (high compute) | 79.4% | 80.2% |
| Terminal-bench | 43.2% | not published |

SWE-bench Verified measures whether a model can resolve real GitHub issues in open-source Python projects. The "high compute" figures use parallel attempts with a selection step, so they are not directly comparable with the standard ones.

## Price

| Model | Input (per million tokens) | Output (per million tokens) |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Opus 4 | $15 | $75 |
| Claude Sonnet 4 | $3 | $15 |

Both were available through the Claude apps, the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud's Vertex AI.

## What was new besides the scores

- **Extended thinking with tool use (beta):** both models could alternate between reasoning and calling tools such as web search, instead of thinking first and acting afterwards.
- **Parallel tool use**, and in Sonnet 4's case, more precise instruction following.
- **Memory files:** when given access to local files, Opus 4 could write and update notes to keep track of long tasks.
- **Fewer shortcuts:** Anthropic said both models were 65% less likely than Sonnet 3.7 to use shortcuts or loopholes on agentic tasks prone to them.
- **Claude Code became generally available**, with integrations for VS Code, JetBrains and GitHub Actions.

## Safety level

Anthropic said the release included "implementing measures for higher AI Safety Levels like ASL-3", a stricter tier of safeguards under its Responsible Scaling Policy.

## What it meant

The launch made coding agents the center of Anthropic's pitch: the headline numbers were software-engineering benchmarks, and Claude Code shipped as a full product the same day. Benchmarks published by a model's maker are a starting point, not a verdict; independent evaluations and real use on your own codebase are a better guide.
